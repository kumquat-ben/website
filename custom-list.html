Custom list Q&A

How custom Kumquat list requests work.

What is a custom business list?

A custom business list is a CSV dataset built around a specific niche, geography, record count, and set of required fields.

What should the request include?

Include the niche or industry, location, target record count, required fields, budget, timeline, and examples of ideal businesses.

Why is there a deposit?

The $99 deposit helps confirm the request is serious before Athena reviews the scope and follows up with next steps.

How does Athena follow up?

After checkout, Athena reviews the request details and follows up by email about feasibility, timeline, and delivery.
