The problem

The Problem

Businesses do not have a data shortage. They have a structured-data problem. The public internet contains millions of useful business signals, but turning those signals into usable datasets usually requires developers, scraping infrastructure, manual cleanup, enrichment, analysis, and compliance review.

The solution

The Solution

Kumquat uses AI-assisted crawler generation, public-source research, data cleaning, and human review to turn messy web signals into ready-to-use business datasets. Customers can buy existing CSV lists or request custom datasets by niche, city, industry, signal, buyer type, or workflow.

Why now

Why Now

AI agents need structured inputs. Sales agents, research agents, marketing agents, and workflow automation tools are only as useful as the data they operate on. Kumquat gives those agents clean, targeted, commercial datasets they can act on.

The wedge

The Wedge

Kumquat focuses on high-intent niche datasets with opportunity labels. Instead of generic lead lists, Kumquat identifies why a business may be useful to contact, such as no online ordering, no booking link, weak SEO, broken contact form, ad spend signal, hiring signal, or sponsor signal.

Data process

How Kumquat Builds Data

1

Define the market

We define the buyer segment, geography, category, opportunity signal, and required fields.

2

Generate or adapt crawlers with AI agents

AI agents and Codex-assisted workflows help create focused collection workflows for each dataset type.

3

Collect public business signals

We gather public business signals from websites, directories, business pages, search results, and open web sources.

4

Clean and normalize the data

Records are deduplicated, normalized, formatted, and prepared for downstream sales and research workflows.

5

Add opportunity labels

Where useful, records are tagged with commercial context such as booking gaps, website gaps, ad signals, hiring signals, or sponsor signals.

6

Human-review the dataset where needed

Human review helps check edge cases, unclear records, source quality, and category fit.

7

Deliver CSV, spreadsheet, API-ready, or agent-ready files

Customers receive structured files that can support sales teams, CRMs, enrichment workflows, or AI agents.

8

Refresh important datasets on a schedule

Monitors and scheduled refreshes help keep priority datasets useful as public sources change.

Responsible use

Compliance and Responsible Use

Kumquat focuses on public business websites, business contact pages, public company information, source URLs, professional-use datasets, and last-verified dates where available. Kumquat is not building consumer surveillance. We focus on public-source business information, commercial signals, source transparency, removal/correction workflows, and responsible use.
