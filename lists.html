Catalog Q&A

Business list questions, answered directly.

What does Kumquat sell?

Kumquat sells downloadable CSV datasets of niche business prospects for sales outreach, market research, partnerships, sponsorship, software sales, and agency prospecting.

What list categories are available?

The catalog includes website opportunity, software gap, ad spend signal, event and venue, professional services, real estate and property, creator intelligence, public organization, local sponsorship, and other high-signal business datasets.

What fields can be included?

Fields vary by product and may include business name, website, city, state, category, opportunity signal, phone, public email, contact page, social link, landing page, source URL, followers, average views, activity, sponsor signals, fit scores, and notes.

How are files delivered?

After payment, a download link is delivered by email once the dataset is available. Delivery may take a few seconds, up to 24 hours, or longer if manual processing is needed.
