FAQ

Questions before you buy

What do I get after buying?

You receive a downloadable CSV file or spreadsheet with the fields listed on the product card or product page.

Can I use Kumquat lists with an AI sales agent?

Yes. Kumquat is positioned around lead lists for AI agents, outbound-ready data, sales agent data, and structured CSV lists your agent can use for research, personalization, and outreach workflows.

Are the lists verified?

Each Kumquat list includes a last-verified date and source links when available.

Do you sell private or sensitive data?

No. Kumquat focuses on publicly available business information, public websites, company contact pages, public profiles, source links, and professional-use datasets.

Can I request a custom list?

Yes. Use the custom list page to request custom data lists with the niche, location, record count, required fields, budget, timeline, and notes.

How do I receive the file?

After payment, a download link is delivered by email once the dataset is available. Delivery can take a few seconds, up to 24 hours, or longer if the request requires manual review or processing.
