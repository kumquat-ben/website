FAQ

Questions before you buy

What do I get after buying?

You receive a downloadable CSV file with the fields listed on the product card or product page.

What is a Creator Intelligence List?

It is a CSV of influencers, bloggers, streamers, or other creators organized for sponsorship and partnership outreach, with contact paths, niche data, activity signals, source URLs, and Kumquat scores where available.

Are the lists verified?

Each Kumquat list includes a last-verified date and source links when available.

Do you sell personal data?

Kumquat focuses on business data, public websites, company contact pages, and professional-use datasets.

Can I request a custom list?

Yes. Use the custom list page to submit a paid deposit with the niche, location, record count, required fields, budget, timeline, and notes.

How do I receive the file?

After payment, a download link is delivered by email once the dataset is available. Delivery can take a few seconds, up to 24 hours, or longer if the request requires manual review or processing.
