Compliance

Compliance and acceptable use.

Kumquat provides niche business datasets for lawful business research, market discovery, partnerships, and professional outreach.

Kumquat focuses on:

  • Public business websites
  • Business contact pages
  • Public company information
  • Source URLs
  • Professional-use datasets
  • Last-verified dates where available

Buyers are responsible for:

  • Following applicable email, privacy, advertising, and data laws
  • Respecting opt-out requests
  • Verifying data before use
  • Using relevant and truthful outreach
  • Avoiding deceptive or abusive campaigns

Kumquat does not encourage spam, harassment, scraping abuse, illegal data use, or deceptive outreach.

Compliance Q&A

Plain-language data use answers.

What kind of data does Kumquat focus on?

Kumquat focuses on public business websites, business contact pages, public company information, source URLs, and professional-use datasets.

Who is responsible for outreach compliance?

Buyers are responsible for following applicable email, privacy, advertising, and data laws in the places where they operate.

What should buyers avoid?

Buyers should avoid spam, harassment, deceptive outreach, abusive campaigns, illegal data use, and ignoring opt-out requests.

Should buyers verify data before use?

Yes. Buyers should verify data before use because public business information can change after a dataset is prepared.
